Important operating principle
Branch presidents may review these materials and prepare local training plans. Official IPMA qualifications, instructor certifications, rank certifications and certificates remain subject to World Headquarters procedures and approval. Before issuing an official certificate or presenting a new program as an IPMA-certified curriculum, please obtain headquarters approval.
