Houston Branch — First Operating Steps

  1. Review the training systems and available official materials before announcing a local IPMA program.
  2. Prepare a simple Houston training plan: target group, subject, date, venue and instructor.
  3. Send the proposed seminar or course plan to IPMA World Headquarters for review when it will be presented as an official IPMA activity.
  4. IPMA qualifications, instructor certifications, rank certifications and official certificates remain subject to World Headquarters procedures and approval.
  5. Do not issue a new curriculum, qualification or certificate as officially IPMA-certified until headquarters approval has been received.
