Recommended Houston Branch Starting Sequence

  1. Confirm the Houston Branch official registry profile.
  2. Review this English Programs & Training Guide and the English Training Materials page.
  3. Select a first activity appropriate to the local audience and instructor competence.
  4. Prepare a short proposal with target group, subject, date, venue and instructor.
  5. Submit the proposal to IPMA World Headquarters before presenting it as an officially approved IPMA seminar or certificate course.
  6. After headquarters review, promote and operate the approved activity using the authorized IPMA identity and materials.
